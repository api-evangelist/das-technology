DAS Technology (formerly Digital Air Strike) is an automotive-retail marketing and customer-experience technology company serving more than 9,300 dealerships and OEMs. Its AI-native CDXP (Customer Data and Experience Platform) unifies sales, service, and retention data across three product lines: Attract (multi-channel digital advertising across Google, Facebook, Microsoft, Amazon, and TikTok), Convert (AI-powered lead response, instant quotes, and long-cycle nurturing), and Retain (reputation management, equity mining, and service-retention campaigns). DAS Technology ships a real REST API — the DAS Web API — served from its legacy Digital Air Strike domain and documented by two publicly readable Swagger 2.0 documents covering 136 operations across accounts and dealer-group hierarchy, reviews and reputation, surveys and NPS, enterprise statistics, transactional messaging, users, vehicles and transactions. The contract is discoverable but the program around it is not: there is no developer portal, no pricing, no SDK, no sandbox, no status page and no changelog, and access is arranged through the dealer, OEM or partner relationship.

**Areas:** Company · MarTech · Automotive · Automotive Retail · Marketing · Customer Data Platform · Advertising · Reputation Management · Reviews · Surveys · Customer Experience · Dealership
